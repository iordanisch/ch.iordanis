My Design Process

A systematic approach to solving complex problems through design thinking and user-centered methodologies.

1. Discover

User research, stakeholder interviews, competitive analysis, and defining the problem space.

  • User Interviews
  • Survey Analysis
  • Persona Creation

2. Define

Synthesizing research insights, creating user journeys, and establishing design requirements.

  • Journey Mapping
  • Problem Statements
  • Success Metrics

3. Ideate

Brainstorming solutions, sketching concepts, and exploring multiple design directions.

  • Crazy 8s
  • Storyboarding
  • Concept Testing

4. Prototype

Building wireframes, high-fidelity mockups, and interactive prototypes for testing.

  • Wireframing
  • UI Design
  • Interactive Prototypes

5. Test & Iterate

Usability testing, gathering feedback, and refining designs based on real user data.

  • Usability Testing
  • A/B Testing
  • Design Handoff

Wood Mackenzie

ENHANCING DATA EXPLORATION IN WOOD MACKENZIE’S PLATFORM


This project focused on improving chart interactions in Wood Mackenzie’s Lens platform by identifying where users struggled. Through user research, analytics, session recordings, and heuristic evaluations, guided by Double Diamond and Lean UX, UX improvements enhanced discoverability, clarified mental models, reduced friction, and increased engagement with complex data.

COMPLETE RE DESIGN OF THE PLATFORM. NEW UI GUIDE LINES AND UX IMPROVEMENTS.


As the product lead my responsibility was to improve the product and find out what works and what doesn't for the users.
